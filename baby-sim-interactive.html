Your patternYou already know the warm response. The hard part is reaching for it under stress — being tired, rushed, or worried about mess are the most common triggers. Noticing that is the first step.
Try this next time
  1. PauseTake one breath before you speak.
  2. Name the feeling“Oh no, it fell! That's frustrating.”
  3. Fix it together“Want to build it again with me?”

Why it works: naming a feeling helps young children calm down, and fixing it together turns a mistake into practice.
